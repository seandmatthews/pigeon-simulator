## Pass 2 (2026-11-08)

No new downloads — the actual Ames SE elevator cluster remains unfilled
under CC. Pass 2 searched Commons ("grain elevator Ames Iowa", the full
Category:Grain elevators in Iowa — all Iowa files are OTHER towns, none
Ames), Flickr-CC ("ames grain elevator" = 0 results), and the web index
(only ARR commercial results). Best view-online pointers recorded:

- Five Star Cooperative photos (ARR, cooperative operator at the Ames-area
  facilities): https://www.fivestarcoop.com
- Google Street View along S Duff Ave at the UP underpass SE of downtown —
  the cluster's silos/annex bins rise directly east of the street there.
- The FEMA 2010 flood series on Commons (PD) shows the S Duff corridor but
  not the elevators.

Iowa-generic typology references in this cluster (10-12) remain the shape/
material basis; the Ames cluster's distinguishing traits for the modeler:
adjacent to the UP main, tall pale silo bank + steel annex bins + attached
headhouse, visible from S Duff and the ISU research farms SE of town.
