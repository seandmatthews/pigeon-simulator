# Zebra crossing — research note (T-102)

## The facts that matter for the game

- **Stripe orientation:** zebra stripes run ALONG the road (parallel to traffic
  flow). Pedestrians step over them like rungs; the bars repeat ACROSS the road.
  (Singapore layout diagram; every photo.) The in-game T-069 bands have this
  INVERTED — transverse bars repeating along the road.
- **Bar proportions (UK spec):** bars ~0.5 m wide with ~0.5 m gaps; crossing
  band length (along the road) ~2.4-3 m; the band spans the full road width.
- **Placement:** bands sit on each intersection approach, clear of the corner-
  cutting sweep area of turning vehicles; often paired with a stop/give way line.

## What the game should copy vs simplify

Copy: orientation (along-road bars), band at each approach spanning the road,
bar/gap rhythm roughly 1:1. Simplify: no zig-zag approach markings, no belisha
beacons, no stop lines (T-069 scope), single flat white.
